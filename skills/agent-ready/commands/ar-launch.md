description: Go through the before-you-go-live checklist together.
---
Go through `docs/operator/launch.md` with the operator before going live.

Tick what you can check yourself: `node .agents/scripts/health.mjs`, the security list in `docs/security.md`, phone widths and keyboard use, limits already recorded. For everything else, add an entry to `docs/operator/todo.md` (things they do) or `docs/operator/mtest.md` (things they try), with plain steps.

Finish with a short list: what is ready, what is not ready yet and why, and what they need to do first.
